Head FAQ — what is each head trying to notice?

19 policy inputs explained
Looking for “are we going to lose?” Use outcome_distribution and value for the whole game. For danger before our next decision, look at opponent_response → knockout; for the next few decisions, use tactical_outcomes → own knockout. Despite its name, lethal_threat asks whether we can take a prize soon—it is offensive danger to the opponent.

A head is one learned opinion the model uses alongside the others. Its description says what it was trained to predict; it does not prove that the head mattered on the selected step. “Current decision effect” below is the exact policy change when that one head is removed while everything else stays fixed. Head effects are nonlinear and should not be added together as percentages.
