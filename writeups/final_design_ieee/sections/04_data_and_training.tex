\section{Data and Training Lineage}

\subsection{Eligibility and split isolation}

Revision 9 selected the most recent 20 contiguous UTC days, 23 July through 11
August 2026. Raw episodes are scanned, but derivative rows are restricted at
the acting-seat level. A decision is eligible if and only if that seat's exact
recorded setup deck contains literal card ID 743. This is a membership test,
not an archetype classifier: a tech copy qualifies, and a deck label cannot
substitute for the card record. If only one seat qualifies, the other seat's
decisions are excluded. Each included row retains a digest of the complete
acting-seat setup-deck multiset so list variants can be stratified without
becoming hidden runtime inputs.

The sealed corpus contains 24,335 games, 2,351,208 decision occurrences,
18,412,973 option records, and 68 acting-deck variants. Whole UTC days are
assigned 14/3/3 to train, validation, and evaluation. The source manifests
report zero cross-split overlap by source archive, day, game, and group. The
evaluation split is unavailable for fitting, support thresholds, scaling, or
tuning. Missing or malformed acting-deck evidence causes explicit exclusion,
not guessed eligibility.

Targets come from recorded selected actions and causally aligned public
trajectories. The system does not fabricate labels for unchosen actions.
Counterfactual simulator data, if later required, must be a separately
identified corpus with public-information restoration seeds and its own
provenance. Incomplete, ambiguous, nonmonotone, or terminal-censored intervals
are masked rather than assigned zero.

\subsection{Full-model bootstrap}

The candidate structure maps exact-name/shape-compatible r195 tensors into the
r274 head architecture plus bug-fixed derivative paths. All twenty day archives
are freshly packed against this structure; an older pre-fix tensor pack cannot
substitute. Training runs 25 genuine full-corpus epochs, and each epoch visits
every eligible decision occurrence once before the next epoch. Semantic-pattern
deduplication may support analysis but cannot replace optimizer rows.

All architecture-present non-combo weights are optimizer-eligible under the
revision-10 override. Combo-state bytes remain physical only to preserve an
existing checkpoint/pack ABI: their loss is zero and their training and runtime
routes are disabled. A prior short, sidecar-only run is preserved as invalid
historical evidence and cannot seed self-play, routing, or promotion. This
negative provenance is important because a valid checkpoint is defined by its
data and optimizer scope, not just by a finite loss.

\subsection{Direct-policy self-play schedule}

After validation and one checksum-bound initial package, the derivative enters
20 direct-policy iterations. Each iteration collects exactly 8,196 source
games: 1,024 self-play and 7,172 against a checksum-pinned public-package mix.
Seats are balanced. At iterations 5, 10, 15, and 20, the loop seals collection
and checkpoint state, then runs five full expert-refresh epochs on the same
recent-20-day pack with the same non-combo optimizer scope. Kaggle packages are
queued only at iterations 10 and 20. There is no iteration 21.

Every shard, pack, checkpoint, optimizer state, opponent package, seed, seat,
and boundary action is content-addressed. A remote worker may produce games,
but the Inzi Blackwell host remains the sole learner and optimizer. Duplicate
suppression and unique lease/game identities prevent one returned game from
appearing twice. A host fault reduces eligible capacity; it does not silently
change the checkpoint, package, or exact retained count.

\subsection{Trainer-only Prize-plan canary}

The Prize-plan v2 sidecar trains horizons $h\in\{1,3,6,12\}$ on complete,
recorded, chosen action programs. Public remaining-Prize counts define a
train-split-only monotone potential
\begin{equation}
  \Phi(p_{\mathrm{ours}},p_{\mathrm{opp}})
    =2P_{\mathrm{iso}}(\mathrm{win}\mid p_{\mathrm{ours}},p_{\mathrm{opp}})-1.
\end{equation}
The potential cannot decrease when our remaining count falls and cannot
increase when the opponent's remaining count falls. A receipt-bound fixed
$\gamma$ produces segment reward
\begin{equation}
  r_t^{P}=\gamma\Phi(s_{t+1})-\Phi(s_t),
\end{equation}
a potential-based shaping form related to policy-invariant reward
transformations~\cite{ng1999policy}. The current actor canary uses only H3:
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
 A_{\mathrm{H3}}(s,a) ={}& z-V_{\mathrm{existing}}(s)\\
 &+0.025\,m_3c_3\left(Q_{\mathrm{plan}}^3(s,a)-V_{\mathrm{plan}}^3(s)\right).
\end{split}
\label{eq:h3}
\end{equation}
Here $m_3=1$ only when all three complete same-seat segments are causal,
unambiguous, and not terminal-censored. The scalar $c_3\in[0,1]$ is a frozen,
receipt-bound public confidence/action-support value fit on training data only.
The one complete-action advantage is broadcast identically across its selected
factorized stages. Sidecar predictions are precomputed and frozen for an actor
unit; no actor gradient reaches the sidecar.

The legacy branch is exactly $z-V_{\mathrm{existing}}(s)$. Any identity,
alignment, support, calibration, finiteness, effective-sample-size, clipping,
or noninterference failure restores that branch at a clean boundary without
rewriting the policy checkpoint. H1, H6, and H12 remain diagnostic under H3
mode with actor coefficient zero; simultaneous horizon terms are forbidden.
