FUTURE-ONLY ACTIVATION BOUNDARY

This file supplies the already reviewed heuristic design behind
config/deck_guides/slowking.yaml. It does not itself create a selector entry,
matchup route, runtime registration, service, bootstrap, or gradient
authority. Archaludon ex remains strictly next. Slowking stays fail-closed
until its exact corpus, route, combo-state head, parameter accounting,
terminal preflight, and checksum-bound validation receipts pass.

The owner's screenshot gives names and counts but no set codes. This proposal
assumes the same identities as the human pilot:

- Slowking SCR 58 / Slowpoke SCR 57;
- Annihilape SSP 100 (Destined Fight);
- Conkeldurr TWM 105 (Gutsy Swing);
- Kyurem SFA 47 (Trifrost);
- Smoochum SSP 75 (Delightful Kiss);
- Latias ex SSP 76;
- Mega Kangaskhan ex MEG 104;

If the exact set-coded export disagrees, every card-specific heuristic in this
proposal must fail closed until re-reviewed. Do not route by name alone where
multiple current cards share a name.


PURPOSE

Propose sparse auxiliary preferences that can be evaluated from the exact
current player-observable state and supplied legal actions. The proposal is
deliberately smaller than the human guide. It labels only local sequencing,
setup, energy, prevention, and attack-selection decisions whose relevant
facts are already known.

It does not predict the opponent, declare whole matchups solved, choose from
hidden deck contents, or assign hindsight labels from the eventual winner.


OBSERVATION WHITELIST

A proposed label may use only:

- the exact legal action candidates supplied at the current decision;
- the acting player's current hand;
- both public boards: Active, Bench, evolution stage, printed identity, HP,
  damage counters, Special Conditions, attached cards, Tools, and visible
  effects;
- both public discard piles and Lost Zones;
- current Stadium and other public global effects;
- current Prize counts, never face-down Prize identities;
- current turn number, player-to-act, first/second status, and whether the
  turn's Supporter, attachment, retreat, Stadium Ability, Pokémon Abilities,
  or named once-per-turn effects have been used;
- current deck size as a count, not its unrevealed order or contents;
- a top-deck card only when exact current-state provenance proves it was
  placed by Academy at Night or Ciphermaniac's Codebreaking and no later draw
  or shuffle invalidated that fact;
- card identities exposed by a currently resolving legal search; and
- exact deterministic damage/effect calculations from the visible state.

The fixed sixty may define which card identities are in scope, but it must not
be used to pretend the current hidden deck or face-down Prizes are known.


FORBIDDEN INPUTS

Every heuristic must abstain rather than use:

- the opponent's hidden hand;
- either player's face-down Prize identities;
- unrevealed deck order or the next random draw;
- hidden cards remaining in the opponent's deck;
- a target presumed available only because it started in the sixty;
- opponent archetype labels as a substitute for visible cards;
- assumed tech cards, future evolution, future Stadium, future Hammer result,
  future coin flip, or future Special Condition;
- a later game state, rollout result, return, winner, or gate outcome;
- private match metadata unavailable to both players;
- recursive Seek Inspiration behavior without a current authoritative ruling;
- an inferred copied-card set identity when the set-coded identity is
  unresolved; or
- the owner's displayed 66.2% result as an action label.


OUTPUT SHAPE

Each eligible rule emits:

- family: setup | sequence | energy | attack_route | prevention | recovery
- proposal_id: stable identifier below
- preferred_action_ids: one or more exact current legal actions
- disfavored_action_ids: only when invalidation is deterministic
- confidence: exact | narrow_inference
- evidence_ids: source anchors
- reason_code: compact machine-auditable reason
- mask: 1 only when every eligibility condition is proved

If more than one rule applies, this proposal does not define a universal
priority override. Exact game-winning legal actions remain the policy's job.
Conflicting preferences must be masked unless a later audited combiner
resolves them.


GLOBAL ABSTENTION RULE

Emit mask=0 when:

- any referenced card identity is ambiguous;
- the legal-action schema does not expose the required target or effect;
- damage, Weakness, Resistance, prevention, Prize, or simultaneous-win
  resolution is incomplete;
- the preference depends on comparing unknown future draws;
- a required once-per-turn flag is missing;
- a top-card provenance flag is missing or stale;
- multiple proposed rules conflict; or
- an action is merely “usually good” rather than locally justified.


PROPOSED CAUSAL HEURISTICS

H01 - PRESERVE A PROVED SEEK TARGET

Family: sequence
Confidence: exact

Eligibility:

- the current top card is known from Academy or Ciphermaniac provenance;
- no draw or shuffle occurred after that placement;
- the card is an identified non-Rule-Box Pokémon;
- a current Slowking can legally use Seek Inspiration; and
- no mandatory action must happen before the attack.

Preference:

- prefer the legal Seek Inspiration action;
- deterministically disfavor optional draw or shuffle actions that would
  consume or randomize the proved target.

Abstain when:

- the attack is not legal;
- the top card was not provenance-tracked;
- an exact current winning action competes; or
- the source card's chosen copied attack is unresolved.

Reason code: preserve_known_seek_source
Evidence: E1, E4, E5


H02 - INVALIDATE TOP KNOWLEDGE AFTER DRAW OR SHUFFLE

Family: sequence
Confidence: exact

Eligibility:

- a top-card fact currently has Academy or Ciphermaniac provenance; and
- the selected legal action draws the top card or shuffles the deck.

State update proposal:

- clear the top-card identity and all downstream attack-source labels unless
  the same resolving action explicitly establishes a new known top.

This is a state/mask rule, not a gameplay preference.

Reason code: top_provenance_invalidated
Evidence: E4, E5, E6, E7


H03 - USE RUN ERRAND BEFORE THE FINAL TOP LOCK

Family: sequence
Confidence: narrow_inference

Eligibility:

- Mega Kangaskhan ex is Active;
- Run Errand is enabled and unused;
- Team Rocket's Watchtower or another visible effect is not disabling it;
- deck size is at least two;
- no known essential Seek target is currently on top; and
- no exact current-state penalty from drawing is represented.

Preference:

- prefer Run Errand before Academy's final target placement.

Abstain when:

- top-card provenance is known and should be preserved;
- deck size is below two;
- Watchtower disables the Ability;
- drawing would trigger an exact loss condition; or
- another exact rule conflicts.

Reason code: run_errand_before_stack
Evidence: E10, E15, E16


H04 - CIPHERMANIAC / RUN ERRAND / ACADEMY BRIDGE

Family: sequence
Confidence: exact after search resolution

Eligibility:

- Ciphermaniac has resolved and the two exact chosen cards are known on top;
- Mega Kangaskhan ex is Active with Run Errand legal;
- Academy at Night is in play and unused this turn, or one of the two known
  cards is Academy and playing it after Run Errand will be legal;
- one drawn card will be an identified non-Rule-Box attack source;
- a current Slowking can legally attack after a public/legal pivot; and
- no shuffle will occur after Run Errand.

Preference sequence:

1. Run Errand to draw the two known cards.
2. Use Academy to put the chosen attack source back on top.
3. Take the legal pivot to Slowking.
4. Apply H01.

Abstain before Ciphermaniac resolves or if the action interface does not expose
the selected search cards and order. Never infer the target is present in the
hidden deck.

Reason code: cipher_draw_restack_bridge
Evidence: E4, E5, E10, E11, E15


H05 - ACADEMY AS THE FINAL LOCK

Family: sequence
Confidence: exact

Eligibility:

- Academy is in play and unused;
- an identified non-Rule-Box attack source is in hand;
- Slowking can legally use Seek Inspiration this turn;
- all required draw and shuffle actions are complete; and
- the attack route for that source passes one of H13-H17 or is otherwise
  supplied by an exact legal tactical label.

Preference:

- prefer Academy targeting that exact source.

Abstain when the copied attack route is not locally justified. Do not label
“put any Pokémon on top.”

Reason code: academy_final_attack_lock
Evidence: E1, E4


H06 - ESTABLISH A SECOND SLOWPOKE

Family: setup
Confidence: narrow_inference

Eligibility:

- fewer than two total Slowpoke/Slowking lines are in play;
- at least one Bench slot is open;
- an exact current legal action benches Slowpoke;
- no visible effect makes that placement illegal; and
- the placement does not consume a Bench slot already required by a
  higher-confidence exact action.

Preference:

- prefer reaching two total lines over adding an otherwise jobless second
  Mega Kangaskhan or other support Pokémon.

Abstain when:

- damage already marks the proposed Slowpoke for a deterministic immediate
  Knock Out;
- the only open slot is needed by a current exact attack/pivot;
- the choice requires hidden matchup assumptions; or
- two lines already exist.

Reason code: redundant_slowpoke_line
Evidence: E15


H07 - TELEPATHIC PSYCHIC ENERGY SETUP

Family: setup
Confidence: exact after legal search choices are exposed

Eligibility:

- Telepathic Psychic Energy is in hand;
- the manual attachment is unused;
- an identified Psychic Pokémon is a legal attachment target;
- at least one Bench slot is open; and
- the resolving action exposes at least one legal Basic Psychic search target.

Preference:

- when fewer than two Slowpoke/Slowking lines are in play, prefer Slowpoke
  among the exposed legal targets;
- use a second target only when its current role is explicit: second
  Slowpoke, Smoochum for a planned Delightful Kiss, or Latias for a current
  pivot.

Abstain rather than infer the hidden deck contains two targets. Do not select
Mega Kangaskhan, Fezandipiti, Meowth, or Kyurem; they are not Basic Psychic
Pokémon.

Reason code: telepathic_visible_basic_setup
Evidence: E8, E17


H08 - FIRST-ATTACK-TURN DELIGHTFUL KISS

Family: setup
Confidence: narrow_inference

Eligibility:

- Smoochum can legally become or remain Active;
- Delightful Kiss is legal;
- an identified Benched Slowpoke is a legal recipient;
- the resolving deck search exposes two Basic Psychic Energy;
- no current legal attack takes a Prize or prevents an exact immediate loss;
  and
- the recipient can remain a legal evolution target.

Preference:

- prefer Delightful Kiss attaching both Basic Psychic Energy to the intended
  Benched Slowpoke.

Abstain when only one Basic is exposed, when a prize-taking attack is legal,
or when choosing first/second is the decision. This rule does not prescribe a
global turn-order preference.

Reason code: delightful_kiss_visible_acceleration
Evidence: E3, E15


H09 - EVOLVE AN ATTACK-READY SLOWPOKE

Family: setup
Confidence: exact

Eligibility:

- Slowking evolution is a legal current action on an identified Slowpoke;
- that line already has enough attached Energy for Seek Inspiration, or an
  exact remaining legal attachment/Counter Gain action completes it;
- a legal top-lock route exists this turn; and
- the evolution does not violate any visible effect.

Preference:

- prefer the legal Slowking evolution over unrelated optional setup.

Abstain if the top-lock route is unknown, if the attack cannot become legal
this turn, or if an exact alternate action wins.

Reason code: evolve_current_seek_attacker
Evidence: E1


H10 - WONDROUS PATCH COMPLETES A BENCHED ATTACKER

Family: energy
Confidence: exact

Eligibility:

- a Basic Psychic Energy is visibly in the acting player's discard;
- Wondrous Patch is a legal action;
- an identified Benched Psychic Pokémon is a legal target; and
- the attachment deterministically completes Seek Inspiration this turn or
  completes the known next attacker under the current visible state.

Preference:

- prefer Patch to that exact target.

Never target the Active, a non-Psychic Pokémon, Telepathic Psychic Energy, or
Boomerang Energy. Abstain when the attachment is merely speculative future
value.

Reason code: patch_completes_visible_attacker
Evidence: E9


H11 - COUNTER GAIN ENABLES ONE-ENERGY SEEK

Family: energy
Confidence: exact

Eligibility:

- the acting player has more Prize cards remaining than the opponent;
- Counter Gain is in hand and legal to attach;
- an identified Slowking has one Psychic Energy attached;
- Seek Inspiration is otherwise short exactly one Colorless requirement;
- a valid attack source can be locked this turn; and
- no Tool-blocking effect applies.

Preference:

- prefer Counter Gain on that Slowking.

Abstain if Prize counts are tied/ahead, if the attack remains illegal, or if
the label assumes Counter Gain will remain active after Prize counts change.

Reason code: counter_gain_current_seek
Evidence: E12


H12 - BOOMERANG AS A TRIFROST TIE-BREAK

Family: energy
Confidence: narrow_inference

Eligibility:

- an attachment to Slowking is legal;
- the selected current attack route is Kyurem's Trifrost;
- Boomerang Energy and another otherwise equivalent Energy attachment are
  both legal;
- the Colorless requirement is the remaining requirement; and
- no current setup trigger from Telepathic Psychic Energy is being sacrificed.

Preference:

- use Boomerang as a tie-break because Trifrost's attack effect will discard
  it and its own text will return it.

Abstain when:

- the target route is Destined Fight;
- the Energy will be discarded by retreat, Hammer, hand cost, or Knock Out;
- Telepathic setup is currently useful; or
- the comparison is not otherwise equivalent.

Reason code: boomerang_trifrost_return
Evidence: E2, E13, E15


H13 - TRIFROST FOR MULTIPLE IMMEDIATE PRIZES

Family: attack_route
Confidence: exact

Eligibility:

- Kyurem is an identified available top-lock source from hand, current known
  top, discard recovery, or resolving search—not inferred hidden availability;
- Seek Inspiration is legal;
- three legal opposing targets are represented;
- exact current damage resolution is available;
- visible prevention has been applied; and
- Trifrost produces at least two immediate Knock Outs and strictly more
  immediate Prizes than every represented legal non-Trifrost attack route.

Preference:

- prefer the Kyurem top-lock route.

Required masks:

- Flower Curtain removes protected Benched non-Rule-Box Pokémon from damage
  and Knock-Out counts;
- Ancient Bulwark removes all damage when the attacking Slowking has two or
  fewer Energy attached;
- Weakness and Resistance are ignored only for Benched targets; and
- all Energy on Slowking is marked for discard, with only qualifying
  Boomerang return.

Abstain when target selection or prevention cannot be resolved exactly.

Reason code: trifrost_multi_prize_now
Evidence: E2, E14, E18


H14 - GUTSY SWING OVER UNNECESSARY DESTINED FIGHT

Family: attack_route
Confidence: exact

Eligibility:

- Conkeldurr and Annihilape are both identified available top-lock sources;
- exact current damage says copied Gutsy Swing Knocks Out the opposing Active;
- damage is not prevented;
- both routes take the same opponent Prizes; and
- keeping Slowking in play does not trigger an exact worse current result.

Preference:

- prefer Gutsy Swing;
- disfavor Destined Fight for this comparison because it also Knocks Out
  Slowking.

The damage calculation must use Psychic-type Slowking as the attacker, not
Fighting-type Conkeldurr.

Reason code: clean_250_preserves_slowking
Evidence: E1, E19


H15 - DESTINED FIGHT TRADES UP

Family: attack_route
Confidence: exact only with full simultaneous-state resolution

Eligibility:

- Annihilape SSP 100 is an identified available top-lock source;
- Seek Inspiration is legal;
- the opposing Active will yield at least two Prizes;
- copied Gutsy Swing and other locally evaluated damage routes do not Knock
  Out that Active;
- no visible effect prevents effects of attacks;
- the acting player has a Benched Pokémon to promote;
- the opponent has at least two Prize cards remaining; and
- the rules engine fully resolves simultaneous Knock Outs, Prizes, no-Bench
  conditions, and win conditions.

Preference:

- prefer Destined Fight as a one-Prize-for-multiple-Prize trade.

Hard abstention:

- opponent has one Prize remaining;
- either player may satisfy multiple win conditions;
- either player has no Bench;
- effect prevention is unresolved;
- Boomerang-return logic is not explicit; or
- Annihilape printing is ambiguous.

Reason code: destined_fight_exact_trade_up
Evidence: E20, E21


H16 - BASTIODON THREE-ENERGY DAMAGE GATE

Family: prevention
Confidence: exact

Eligibility:

- Bastiodon PBL 62 is visibly on the opponent's Bench;
- Ancient Bulwark is active;
- the proposed route deals damage;
- the attacking Slowking has two or fewer Energy cards attached; and
- a legal third Energy attachment is currently available.

Preference:

- prefer the third attachment when it deterministically makes the selected
  damage attack effective this turn.

Mask:

- all damage-route labels remain off while Slowking has two or fewer attached
  Energy under active Ancient Bulwark.

Counter Gain's cost reduction does not change the attached-Energy count.
Abstain if a separate prevention effect remains.

Reason code: ancient_bulwark_third_energy
Evidence: E18


H17 - SHAYMIN FLOWER CURTAIN DAMAGE MASK

Family: prevention
Confidence: exact

Eligibility:

- Shaymin DRI 10 is visibly in play with Flower Curtain active.

State update proposal:

- mark each opposing Benched non-Rule-Box Pokémon as protected from attack
  damage;
- do not mark the Active or Benched Rule-Box Pokémon protected by this rule;
- recalculate H13 after applying the mask.

This is a damage mask, not a whole-matchup concession label.

Reason code: flower_curtain_bench_mask
Evidence: E14


H18 - REPLACE WATCHTOWER BEFORE COLORLESS ABILITY

Family: prevention
Confidence: exact

Eligibility:

- Team Rocket's Watchtower is the current Stadium;
- an identified Colorless Pokémon's Ability is required now;
- Mega Kangaskhan's Run Errand or Meowth's Last-Ditch Catch is otherwise
  legal;
- Academy at Night is an exact legal Stadium action; and
- replacing the Stadium re-enables the Ability under all visible effects.

Preference:

1. play Academy;
2. then use the re-enabled Ability.

Abstain when another Ability lock remains or the Ability's other conditions
are not met.

Reason code: replace_watchtower_for_colorless_ability
Evidence: E4, E16


H19 - NIGHT STRETCHER ENABLES AN EXACT CURRENT ROUTE

Family: recovery
Confidence: exact

Eligibility:

- Night Stretcher is legal;
- an identified Pokémon or Basic Psychic Energy is visibly in discard;
- returning it to hand enables an exact current-turn top-lock, evolution,
  attachment, or knockout route; and
- all subsequent actions in that route are currently legal.

Preference:

- return the exact blocking resource.

Examples eligible only when fully represented:

- Conkeldurr to hand, Academy to top, Gutsy Swing for an exact Knock Out;
- Annihilape to hand, Academy to top, H15 passes;
- Slowking to hand, legal evolution, current Seek attack;
- Basic Psychic to hand, manual attachment, current Seek attack.

Abstain when the benefit is only future access or when multiple targets have
unresolved strategic tradeoffs.

Reason code: stretcher_unblocks_current_route
Evidence: E22


H20 - MULTI-PRIZE BENCHING NEEDS A CURRENT JOB

Family: setup
Confidence: exact as a positive-label gate

Rule:

- do not emit a positive setup label for benching Mega Kangaskhan ex, Latias
  ex, Fezandipiti ex, or Meowth ex merely because the action is legal.

Permitted positive labels require one exact current job:

- Mega Kangaskhan is part of a represented Active/Run Errand/pivot sequence;
- Latias immediately changes a legal Retreat or pivot cost to zero;
- Meowth's Last-Ditch Catch is enabled and a represented Supporter search is
  used this turn; or
- Fezandipiti's Flip the Script condition is currently satisfied, unused, and
  the draw action is explicitly selected by a higher-level legal planner.

This rule does not automatically disfavor benching; it withholds heuristic
approval because the opponent's future gust and Prize route are strategic.

Reason code: multiprize_bench_requires_job
Evidence: E10, E11, E23, E24, E15


H21 - BOOMERANG RETURN CONDITION MASK

Family: energy
Confidence: exact

State update:

- mark Boomerang Energy to return only when the currently resolving attack
  effect used by the Pokémon it is attached to discards that card;
- do not mark return after Hammer, retreat, hand discard, Tool/Ability
  discard, Knock Out, or Destined Fight's Knock Out.

This prevents false energy-forecast labels.

Reason code: exact_boomerang_trigger
Evidence: E13


H22 - ACTIVE-ONLY AND BASIC-ONLY MOBILITY MASK

Family: sequence
Confidence: exact

State update:

- Run Errand is eligible only while Mega Kangaskhan ex is Active;
- Skyliner sets Retreat Cost to zero only for Basic Pokémon in play;
- never apply Skyliner's zero Retreat to Slowking;
- never apply Wondrous Patch to the Active.

This is an eligibility mask, not an action preference.

Reason code: exact_position_and_stage_constraints
Evidence: E9, E10, E11


EXPLICITLY EXCLUDED HUMAN-GUIDE IDEAS

The following remain human strategic advice and must not become labels from
this proposal:

- “always go first” or “always go second”;
- matchup-name-based attack choice;
- a universal two- or three-Slowpoke rule after the opening;
- direct Fezandipiti Cruel Arrow into Shaymin;
- direct Mega Kangaskhan Rapid-Fire Combo;
- Meowth Tuck Tail to remove a Prize liability;
- Slowpoke Dangle Tail recovery when several targets compete;
- Smoochum as a copied attack source;
- Super Psy Bolt by discarding a Slowking;
- Tantrum and its Confusion tradeoff;
- recursive Seek Inspiration;
- speculative one-Trifrost-plus-one-Gutsy-Swing two-turn plans;
- playing around an unseen Hammer, Watchtower, Shaymin, Special Red Card, or
  Bastiodon;
- interpreting absence from an unobserved deck as a face-down Prize;
- generic hand-size thresholds for Meowth or Fezandipiti; and
- any heuristic weight based on the displayed 66.2% result.


PROPOSED AUDIT CASES

Any later offline validator should include at least:

1. Academy target on top, then optional Ultra Ball: H01 prefers attack and
   H02 invalidates top knowledge if Ultra Ball is selected.
2. Ciphermaniac stacks Conkeldurr plus Academy, active Mega Kangaskhan:
   H04 draws both, replays Academy, and preserves the exact target.
3. Ciphermaniac target on top without Academy: Run Errand is not labeled.
4. Watchtower active: Run Errand and Last-Ditch Catch are masked until
   Academy replaces it.
5. Telepathic Psychic Energy attaches to Slowpoke with two open slots:
   only exposed legal Basic Psychic targets are considered.
6. Smoochum with two exposed Basic Psychic and Benched Slowpoke: H08 labels
   Delightful Kiss only when no prize-taking attack exists.
7. Basic Psychic in discard, Benched one-Energy Slowking: H10 labels Patch
   only if it completes a known attack route.
8. Player behind, one-Psychic Slowking, Counter Gain in hand: H11 labels only
   when the top-lock route is also legal.
9. Two 70-HP Benched targets and no prevention: H13 labels Trifrost.
10. Shaymin protects those same non-Rule-Box targets: H17 masks them and H13
    abstains.
11. Two Rule-Box targets behind Shaymin: H17 does not protect them.
12. Bastiodon Benched and Slowking has two Energy: damage routes are masked.
13. Third attachment under Bastiodon: H16 may enable the current damage route.
14. Gutsy Swing 250 takes the Active Knock Out: H14 prefers it over Destined
    Fight.
15. Active three-Prize Pokémon survives 250, opponent has two or more Prizes,
    both Benches nonempty, no protection: H15 may label Destined Fight.
16. Opponent has one Prize: H15 always abstains.
17. Trifrost discards Boomerang: H21 returns it.
18. Crushing Hammer discards Boomerang: H21 does not return it.
19. Destined Fight Knocks Out Slowking with Boomerang attached: H21 does not
    return it.
20. Latias in play and Slowking Active: H22 does not grant free Retreat.

MECHANICS AND EVIDENCE GAPS

- The exact set-coded sixty is not available; card-name ambiguity remains.
- No controlled turn-order study for the exact list was found.
- No authoritative ruling found in this review resolves recursive Seek
  Inspiration; it is excluded.
- Destined Fight requires a complete simultaneous-win and effect-prevention
  resolver; absent that resolver, H15 must always abstain.
- The current Pitch Black interaction set is young. Bastiodon is handled
  from printed text, not matchup outcome data.
