## Buneary / Mega Lopunny ex

When the opponent reveals Buneary, plan for Mega Lopunny ex and a heavy Mist
Energy count. Preserve Enhanced Hammer for a visible Mist Energy, especially
when it is attached to Mega Lopunny ex. Mist Energy prevents Powerful Hand's
attack effect, so remove it before attacking that Pokémon or gust around the
protected target when that produces the winning prize line.

Mega Lopunny ex has 330 HP and gives up three Prize cards. Powerful Hand needs
17 cards in hand to knock it out from full HP. When two Mega Lopunny ex are
available as the opponent's six-Prize route, prioritize two one-hit knockouts.
Avoid a partial hit when the opponent can use Wally's Compassion to heal it;
build the exact 17-card hand first unless a different line ends the game
sooner.
